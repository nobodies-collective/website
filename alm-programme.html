What each session is about?

This page tells you what each session will be working on, and what you should walk away with. For the room-by-room timetable, head to the Schedule page.

Sessions marked ⚡️ are for everyone. Parallel sessions run simultaneously, so you’ll need to choose one. All programmed sessions will be hybrid: the link to join the call will be shared in our channels, so join your preferred one via Discord, Telegram or WhatsApp.

Friday 16 October — Evening

17:00 – 18:30 · ⚡ All together On-site only 90 min
Welcome & Year in Review

Opens the weekend with a shared frame: a summary of what came out of the lessons-learned channel, an icebreaker to get the room warmed up, and a rapid-fire overview of where we stand — annual roadmap, finances, asset acquisition. Ellen and Cupcake will also walk everyone through how facilitation works across the weekend, so we all go into sessions with the same expectations.

Covers: Lessons learned · Icebreaker · Annual roadmap · Finance · Asset acquisition · Facilitation guidelines
18:30 – 19:30 · ⚡ All together Hybrid 60 min
Working Group Speed Dating

Each working group gets 30–60 seconds to pitch their topic. No discussion — just a fast map of everything being worked on over the weekend, so you can figure out which sessions matter most to you and plan accordingly. The mic cuts at 60 seconds. Enforced. Played for laughs.

You'll leave with: A clear picture of what's happening across all sessions so you can self-select

Saturday 17 October — Morning

09:30 – 10:30 · ⚡ All together Hybrid 60 min
Event-Time Ops & Org Hub

There's currently no unified nerve centre during event week — information flows ad hoc through Malfare and Production, stretching both teams beyond their scope. This session designs what that hub actually looks like: where it sits on site, who staffs it, how radio handover works, and what the ops board looks like so anyone can answer "who do I actually call?"

Goes to everyone because it touches every department. Comes to Vidalia with a concrete blueprint to review, not a blank page.

Working group: Event-Time Ops You'll leave with: An agreed site layout and staffing model for the ops hub
Parallel sessions — pick one  10:45 – 11:45
Parallel Hybrid 60 min
Beyond Safehaus: What Should Crew Rest Look Like?

AC cabins covered the heat. People who decompressed off-site came back to strike energised. The second Safehaus never showed up — and we made it. So the model has already shifted. This session turns that into a deliberate decision: what do we actually want crew rest to look like next year? What do we book, what do we budget, and how do we stop the stigma that stepping off-site means stepping out?

Working group: Safehaus You'll leave with: A crew rest model and a budget line to put forward for 2027
Parallel Hybrid 60 min
Clearing the Air on Sound

The Yellow zone wasn't quiet. We know this. The question is what we actually want to do about it: enforce the existing policy properly, or redraw the acoustic map to reflect reality. This session reviews the updated zone framework and mobile sound rules, aligns on what non-compliance actually looks like, and agrees on a community-held policy to take into 2027.

Working group: Sound Policy You'll leave with: A ratified sound policy with enforcement mechanisms
Open space On-site only 60 min
Open Space

No agenda. No lead. Anyone can bring a topic, find a corner of the room, and see who shows up. If you have something you want to work through that doesn't fit elsewhere in the weekend, this is the slot for it.

Parallel sessions — pick one  12:00 – 13:00
Parallel Hybrid 60 min
Designing Our Site: Org Footprint, Structures and Spaces

Toilet design, sleeping cabins, org infrastructure — the physical site reflects choices about who we're building for, even when those choices happen by default. This session is about making them on purpose: what gets built, for whom, and how we make those decisions better as an org so next year's site actually works for everyone on it.

Working group: Site Design You'll leave with: A set of site design principles and a decision process for 2027
Parallel Hybrid 60 min
MoE/MoN: What Are We Building?

Music of Elsewhere / Music of Nowhere has been a real but loosely structured part of what we do. This session looks at it as a serious part of the org: how it's organised, what it needs to thrive, and what we want it to become. We'll align on a budget approach, a team structure, and a plan for 2027.

Working group: MoE/MoN You'll leave with: A governance model and budget outline for MoE/MoN in 2027
Open space On-site only 60 min
Open Space

Same as above — a second open space slot for self-organised conversations. Bring a topic, find a wall, see who comes.

Saturday 17 October — Afternoon & Evening

15:00 – 16:30 · ⚡ All together Hybrid 90 min
How We're Structured: Roles, Responsibilities & Reporting

We've been building operational complexity without always being clear about who owns what. This session lays out the org map clearly: what Board, coordinator, and department roles are each responsible for, what they're explicitly not responsible for, and what the organisation needs from every department in exchange for real autonomy. The goal is to leave with a shared understanding, not a chart for the sake of it.

Working group: Organisational Structure You'll leave with: A clear, agreed scope for each role tier — what's in and what's out
17:00 – 18:30 · ⚡ All together Hybrid 90 min
Getting Into and Out of Roles

Follows on from the roles session. How do people get into coordinator positions — and how do we handle it when things aren't working? This covers selection culture, fixed terms, shadowing and succession planning, and a care-first approach to deselection: inactivity, performance, conflict, and consent-related situations. The aim is to build a system that feels fair and human, not punitive.

Working group: Organisational Structure You'll leave with: Agreed selection and deselection processes, ready to document
19:00 – 20:00 · Working session Hybrid 60 min
Werkhaus: From Volunteer Camp to Org Infrastructure

Werkhaus has been one of the most important things at Elsewhere — a base for Fluffers, Malfare overflow, and the most invested builders. It's also been held together by last-minute goodwill and implicit understandings. This session decides what Werkhaus looks like as a properly governed system: how leads are selected, what the budget model is, what criteria determine who gets a spot, and where it formally sits within the org.

Working group: Werkhaus Lead: Rory You'll leave with: A Werkhaus governance model and a budget proposal for 2027

Sunday 18 October — Morning

09:30 – 11:00 · ⚡ All together Hybrid 90 min
New Site: Purchase and Infrastructure

A structured input session on the potential site acquisition (El Sitio). The goal isn't to make a purchase decision here — it's to get real numbers on the table. Each department maps what they can confirm, estimate, or still need to quote, so the business case has actual figures in it before any vote is called. Pablo and Ward are leading.

Working group: El Sitio Leads: Pablo / Ward You'll leave with: A department-by-department cost map and a clear list of what still needs quoting
11:30 – 13:00 · ⚡ All together Hybrid 90 min
What We're Taking Home: Outcome Pitches

Each working group gets 5–10 minutes to present what they actually produced over the weekend: the decision made, the plan agreed, or the question they're taking forward with a named owner and a timeline. No new discussions — this is the accountability loop that closes the weekend. Everyone leaves knowing what happens next and who's doing it.

All working groups You'll leave with: A shared record of decisions, owners, and next steps from the whole weekend
