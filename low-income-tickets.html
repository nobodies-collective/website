The low-income ticket application campaign for Elsewhere 2026 is now closed. All applicants have been contacted with a response.

Code redemption — ended

The code-redemption deadline was 14 May 2026. Unused codes ceased to be valid after that date.

Refunds

The policy allowed refunds until 1 June 2026. That deadline has passed.

End of the programme

The 2026 programme set 5 June 2026 as the cutoff for assigning new low-income codes.

A few important things to keep in mind

This was the final round of low-income codes for Elsewhere 2026. Not all applicants received a code, and there was no further round. We’re sorry we couldn’t accommodate everyone.

Thank you for your understanding and for being part of Elsewhere. We work to make this space as accessible, inclusive, and fair as possible for everyone who wants to be part of it. 🖤
