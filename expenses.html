Expense Reimbursement Guide

Coordinators & Volunteers · Elsewhere 2026 · Nobodies Collective Association

Pay upfront, keep your invoice, submit an expense report in the Humans app. Once approved, you receive a SEPA bank transfer. This includes travel reimbursements — same process, just select the relevant budget category.

Before you buy

Check your budget first. Make sure the expense is within your department's approved budget. If unsure, check with your coordinator or Daniela or Peter before purchasing — not after.

Getting the invoice right

Always request a proper invoice (not just a till receipt). The invoice must be addressed to:

EU / UK / International purchases
Same details, but use
VAT Number: ESG26726299 (instead of the NIF)

Tickets (till receipts) are accepted when no invoice is possible, but a proper invoice is always preferable — it lets us recover VAT and keeps our accounting clean.

Meals & fuel

Association card only. Meals and fuel must be paid with an Association card — personal payment won't be reimbursed for these categories. Cards are being set up and will be issued before the event. Contact your department's coordinator if you need to incur these types of expenses.

Submitting your claim

  1. Go to the Humans app → Expense Reports → New Report
  2. Select your budget category
  3. Add expense lines with amounts and attach the invoice/receipt to each line
  4. Set your payment IBAN if you haven't already
  5. Submit for coordinator / Finance Admin review
  6. On approval you receive a SEPA bank transfer
You can group multiple purchases in one report — but each invoice needs its own line. Keep it to one department per report — don't mix categories (e.g. Cantina and Radios in the same report).
No invoice, no reimbursement. Missing or incorrect invoices mean we can't process the reimbursement. When in doubt, ask before you buy.
