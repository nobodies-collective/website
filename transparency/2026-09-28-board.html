Board present: Ben D (secretary, facilitating), Peter D (treasurer), Ward D · Absent: Daniel T, Victor P, Pablo V, Chris O, Michelle · Observers: Daniela (production coordinator), Ellen K Three of the eight board members attended, so no quorum was present; no formal votes were taken. There was no pre-submitted agenda. The meeting ended about ten minutes early.

1. Opening and quorum

  • Ben D noted that, with three board members present, the board could not vote on anything that required quorum. The meeting proceeded as updates and discussion.

2. Expense reports and barrio deposits

  • Expense reports. Individual expense reports are being approved in Humans. Peter D noted that approval of a report comes before, and is separate from, payment.
  • Barrio deposits. Ellen K raised the barrio deposits still to be handled. Peter D explained that each deposit needs a proper owner name in Humans, because Humans generates the invoices through Holded, the association’s accounting software. Some barrios are associations and will need a proper invoice, so they will have to be contacted; Ellen K will take this on. Deposits paid by bank transfer rather than through Humans are already visible in Humans and do not need separate handling.

3. Membership applications

  • The board reviewed pending membership applications. Ben D noted that the backlog cannot be cleared properly until a meeting with quorum.
  • Peter D showed the reorganised admin menu in Humans, now grouped by section (for example, Governance) rather than one long list.

4. Storage and equipment

  • An update was given on storage and equipment; no decisions were taken.

5. Annual Leadership Meeting (ALM): spending authorisation

  • Peter D reported, so that it is on the record, that he has authorised up to €25,000 for the ALM, covering travel reimbursements and the event’s own costs. The amount is close to the limit of what he would normally authorise without the board.
  • Ben D asked what the limit is. Peter D: no written policy yet; the working guideline has been 5% of the overall budget, and this authorisation is within it.

6. Working groups in Humans and the Finances 2027 working group

  • Peter D proposed adding a working-group module to Humans to handle the administrative side of working groups, such as email groups and, where relevant, budgets. Ben D had no objection, and none was raised.
  • Peter D will start the Finances 2027 working group in October, probably the following week, using the new module so that people can join through Humans. It will look at the main levers for the event budget: ticket price, low-income allocation, the allocation for creativity, staffing, travel reimbursements and similar. Daniela intends to join.

7. Working-group budgets and decision-making

  • Ellen K asked how decisions are made in a working group, especially one that holds a budget or that anyone can join. Ben D: as things stand, working groups make recommendations, including budget proposals, which the board then approves or rejects. Ellen K agreed that this raises no issue as long as they only recommend.
  • Peter D clarified that giving a working group a budget was mainly an accounting device, so that expense reports have a category to go against. Most working groups would not hold one. Daniela’s view was that the ALM is an event rather than a working group, and that decisions on spending sit with departments. For example, a working group can develop a concept and present it to the relevant department, which then decides what to allocate from its own budget. Ellen K summarised that working groups should not hold budgets except as an accounting convenience. Daniela added that, ideally, the budget for next year’s ALM would be approved in advance.
  • No decision was taken.

8. Budget categories and planning for 2027

  • Peter D proposed distinguishing between operating expenses (recurring, year-on-year costs such as fuel, water and basic maintenance, which can largely be carried forward and adjusted) and capital improvements (larger one-off projects for which a department applies for money beyond its usual budget). There is not yet a process for capital requests. His suggestion is that a community body, probably the Finances 2027 working group or a separate group, should see the proposals and prioritise them against the money available, according to what the community values most.
  • Ellen K noted that this returns to the question of how such a group makes decisions. Peter D said he hoped the group would reach a decision its members could accept. He proposed a deadline-based approach: if a group has not decided by the date the event timeline requires, he will decide, taking the group’s input into account, so that the event’s deadlines are met.
  • Asked by Ellen K whether finance is a department, Daniela confirmed that it is, and at present a one-person one. Peter D explained that this year’s finance decisions had been kept deliberately conservative, with improvements planned for 2027. Ben D added that some finance functions could be shared among more people.
  • Daniela noted that this overlaps with the organisational-structure working group, which is about to circulate a survey on department roles and tasks, including whether and how any roles should be paid.

9. Department roles in Humans

  • Peter D and Daniela discussed how department roles are set up in Humans. The original assumption was that a department’s coordinator would also handle shifts, finance and other admin; in practice this has not worked for every department. The proposal is that a coordinator can be supported by named roles (for example, a shift manager, a finance manager and a purchasing manager), with the permissions those roles need. Small departments would not need all of them. Some departments, such as creativity (art grants), low-income and ticketing, need features of their own. The organisational-structure working group is also looking at what the coordinator role involves.
  • Timeline. Peter D: most finance work should be complete by mid-October, ahead of the ALM. November and December will go to the budget for next year and to building the additional modules the app needs.

10. Close

  • No further items were raised, and the meeting closed about ten minutes early.
  • Next meeting: next Monday, aiming for quorum.

Decisions

  • No formal decisions were taken (no quorum).
  • ALM spending: Peter D’s authorisation of up to €25,000 for the ALM was reported and recorded.
  • Humans working-group module: no objection was raised to adding it.

Ellen K estuvo de acuerdo en que esto no plantea ningún problema siempre que se limiten a recomendar.

Por ejemplo, un grupo de trabajo puede desarrollar un concepto y presentarlo al departamento correspondiente, que después decide qué asignar desde su propio presupuesto. Ellen K resumió que los grupos de trabajo no deberían tener presupuestos salvo como una conveniencia contable. Daniela añadió que, idealmente, el presupuesto del ALM del próximo año se aprobaría con antelación.

8. Categorías presupuestarias y planificación para 2027

  • Peter D propuso distinguir entre gastos operativos (costes recurrentes, año tras año, como combustible, agua y mantenimiento básico, que en gran medida pueden trasladarse y ajustarse) y mejoras de capital (proyectos puntuales de mayor envergadura para los que un departamento solicita dinero más allá de su presupuesto habitual). Todavía no hay un proceso para las solicitudes de capital. Su sugerencia es que un órgano comunitario, probablemente el grupo de trabajo Finanzas 2027 o un grupo distinto, debería ver las propuestas y priorizarlas frente al dinero disponible, según lo que la comunidad valore más.
  • Ellen K señaló que esto vuelve a la cuestión de cómo decide un grupo así. Peter D dijo que esperaba que el grupo llegara a una decisión que sus miembros pudieran aceptar. Propuso un enfoque basado en plazos: si un grupo no ha decidido para la fecha que exige el calendario del evento, él decidirá, teniendo en cuenta la aportación del grupo, para que se cumplan los plazos del evento.
  • Preguntada por Ellen K sobre si finanzas es un departamento, Daniela confirmó que lo es, y actualmente de una sola persona. Peter D explicó que las decisiones financieras de este año se habían mantenido deliberadamente conservadoras, con mejoras previstas para 2027. Ben D añadió que algunas funciones financieras podrían compartirse entre más personas.

9. Roles de departamento en Humans

  • Peter D y Daniela debatieron cómo se configuran los roles de departamento en Humans. El supuesto original era que el coordinador de un departamento también se encargaría de los turnos, las finanzas y otras tareas administrativas; en la práctica, esto no ha funcionado para todos los departamentos. La propuesta es que un coordinador pueda estar apoyado por roles nombrados (por ejemplo, un responsable de turnos, un responsable de finanzas y un responsable de compras), con los permisos que esos roles necesitan. Los departamentos pequeños no necesitarían todos ellos. Algunos departamentos, como creatividad (becas artísticas), bajos ingresos y venta de entradas, necesitan funciones propias. El grupo de trabajo de estructura organizativa también está estudiando qué implica el rol de coordinador.

8. Categorie di budget e pianificazione per il 2027

  • Peter D ha proposto di distinguere tra spese operative (costi ricorrenti, anno su anno, come carburante, acqua e manutenzione di base, che possono in gran parte essere riportati e adeguati) e miglioramenti di capitale (progetti una tantum più grandi per i quali un dipartimento richiede fondi oltre il proprio budget abituale). Non esiste ancora un processo per le richieste di capitale. La sua proposta è che un organo comunitario, probabilmente il gruppo di lavoro Finanze 2027 o un gruppo separato, esamini le proposte e le dia priorità in base al denaro disponibile, secondo ciò che la comunità valorizza di più.

9. Ruoli di dipartimento in Humans

  • Peter D e Daniela hanno discusso di come i ruoli di dipartimento siano configurati in Humans. L'ipotesi originaria era che il coordinatore di un dipartimento gestisse anche turni, finanze e altre pratiche amministrative; in pratica questo non ha funzionato per tutti i dipartimenti. La proposta è che un coordinatore possa essere supportato da ruoli nominati (ad esempio, un responsabile dei turni, un responsabile delle finanze e un responsabile degli acquisti), con i permessi necessari a quei ruoli. I dipartimenti piccoli non ne avrebbero bisogno di tutti. Alcuni dipartimenti, come la creatività (borse artistiche), il basso reddito e la biglietteria, necessitano di funzionalità proprie. Il gruppo di lavoro sulla struttura organizzativa sta inoltre esaminando cosa comporti il ruolo di coordinatore.
