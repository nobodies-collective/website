Event Dates Sync: Daniel T & Peter K

Date: Wednesday 5 August 2026, in person (Barcelona)Type: Sync note
Participants: Daniel T (board), Peter K
What this is. A sync note from a working conversation between the people named above — not the minutes of a working-group meeting: no agenda was circulated and nobody else was invited. It records only the parts of the conversation that concern the 2027 event-dates decision; unrelated topics and personal remarks are left out. Published with the agreement of the participants, as part of the Event Dates working group’s commitment to keep its work in the open.

An informal conversation whose purpose, for the dates topic, was to ask Peter K — who has long-standing contacts across the European burn community — to lead the outreach to other burns about a possible move of the 2027 event dates. The conversation also covered longer-term ideas for the new site and several governance topics, which are not recorded here.

1. Why the dates question is urgent

Both agreed that the wildfire risk on the current early-July dates has become an event-cancellation risk rather than a discomfort: on the letter of the emergency rules, the 2026 event could have been shut down more than once. Heat is a second, independent reason: several long-standing participants have said they will not return in July.

Daniel T noted that the community may still vote to keep the current dates; he thinks that would be a mistake, but the choice belongs to the members.

2. Candidate windows discussed

  • Late May / early June — Daniel T’s working preference, with early June slightly ahead because late May coincides with Burning Nest (UK).
  • Mid-June — judged only a marginal improvement: two weeks earlier does little for fire risk; a full month does much more, even if wetter.
  • August — ruled out (fire risk worse than July).
  • September — attractive on paper but historically unpopular because of Burning Man travel; Daniel T’s risk compilation also puts early-September fire risk at roughly late-June levels, and late September brings cold winds. Treated as out.
  • A local recommendation relayed to the board advised dropping May and early June (May is historically the wettest month in Monegros and peak harvest season, with heavy machinery on the access roads). Daniel T’s view: keep those weeks on the list and let members weigh the trade-off, since removing them would leave too small a shift to matter for fire risk.

3. Bird nesting

Early July already falls in a high-sensitivity nesting period for protected species; the season is broad (roughly March to October). Preliminary figures came from public sources and were challenged in the conversation as not authoritative — the definitive answer has to come from the environmental authority (INAGA), whose formal response time is long. A request is being filed through the board; Peter K offered to consult a bird biologist he knows in the Lleida area in the meantime.

4. Outreach to other European burns

Daniel T shared a working list of roughly twenty burns to contact. The ask, for each: confirm their 2027 dates, then for the week before, the same week and the week after their event, what the impact of Elsewhere moving there would be, and what they would do in response. The output is a one-page table per candidate week.

Peter K suggested framing any potential clash as an offer rather than a taking — a “cultural exchange”: help the other burn set up a barrio here, and send a delegation to theirs. Both noted that some burns might welcome early July becoming free. Burning Nest is the main one to talk to; a date swap was floated as a possibility to raise with them.

Daniel T also suggested inviting other burns to poll their own members about our dates, so that the eventual choice is visibly informed by the wider community.

5. Process and timeline

General Assembly expected mid-September; answers from other burns wanted by end of August so they can go into the information pack. A dedicated channel on Discord is the place for the conversation. Daniel T to publish a preliminary information page so people can read the data rather than a spreadsheet.
