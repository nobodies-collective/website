Sound Policy Working Group

Status: RegisteredReporting date: 28 February 2027
Purpose. To update the sound policy for Elsewhere to meet the evolution of the event.
Expected output. A draft of an updated version of the sound policy.
Where it stands (25 November 2026). The draft has become the Elsewhere Sound Guide 2027 (v0.3): a plain-language guide for participants rather than a policy document. In short:
  • Know your zone. The zones apply to everyone: barrios, freecampers, art and art cars, battery-powered or not.
  • Register larger setups (PA, subs, big battery speakers, drum kits) through Barrio Support or Creativity.
  • Point speakers outward, towards deep playa. Large dance-floor installations go to the Red perimeter; Red dance floors have a dB meter.
  • Build: agree times with neighbours, music off by 02:00 and not loud before 09:00.
  • Problems: talk first, then Nomads or Malfare. A warning needs a rule actually broken; after two warnings a lead may stop the sound, with the Power team.
Still open: the dB limits and hours for each zone, sound on the playa and in freecamping, art car areas, meter settings, and who follows up reports. Next: v0.3 goes out for comment, then to barrio leads and a community call (provisionally early December).

The second draft: the two-warning process, yellow as a daytime zone, speaker direction checked before opening, one set of sound questions for barrios, art and art cars, and zones on the playa.
10 November 2026Working group call

From the first draft to a simpler guide

Feedback that the first draft was too long and too bureaucratic, and the questions it raised: registering sound systems, the zones, large installations, build and strike, reporting, and when sound may be stopped.
17 October 2026ALM session

ALM — Clearing the Air on Sound

A structured session at the Annual Leadership Meeting on the open questions: barrio sound rules, art piece and art car policy, consistent enforcement, and support for nomads handling sound complaints — aiming at a formal proposal before city planning starts barrio placement. The outcome write-up will follow on the ALM outcomes page.
1 October 2026Community call

Report at the community call (Cocci)

Complaints about sound came from all over the site: music audible in the green zone, no policy at all for the RV area (including generators and engines), and too little difference between the yellow, orange and red zones. Ideas under discussion include a dedicated, recognisable sound role and how to handle camps and art cars that ignore the policy.
