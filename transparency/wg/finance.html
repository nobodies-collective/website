Finance 2027 Working Group

Status: active since October 2026; open to anyone (join through Humans)Convenor: Peter D
Purpose. To work out the association’s finances for 2027: the ticket price, the allocation for low-income and other special tickets, the budget for art, and the other main budget levers — staffing, travel reimbursements and similar — tied to ticket sales.
Expected output. A recommended 2027 budget, built as a decision tree tied to ticket sales (the ticketing strategy is its income side), to be put to a General Assembly in mid-December 2026. Meets weekly on Mondays.

Record

October 19, 2026 Finance Working Group

Finance 2027 Working Group — First Session: Scope, Timeline and Ticketing

The first session of the new working group. The working aim is a recommended 2027 budget, built as a decision tree tied to ticket sales, for a General Assembly vote in mid-December (date not yet set). Ticket pricing is part of the group’s work, as the income side of the budget, and Daniel T will lead community engagement on it. First ideas, for discussion only: a pay-what-you-can slider with a visible budget, a low starting price that rises over time, a refund pool, instalments, low-income tickets linked to sales, and community-ranked event improvements. No prices or budget figures were agreed.

Present: Peter D (treasurer, convenor), Daniel T (president), Daniela (production coordinator), Cocci
October 18, 2026 ALM Open Space

ALM Open Space — Ticketing

An open discussion at the Annual Leadership Meeting; no decisions were taken, and any prices or numbers mentioned were ideas only. Participants broadly agreed that more low-income tickets are needed, even at a somewhat higher low-income price, with a longer window and some late or reserved tickets. Also discussed: a refund queue through the official sale, separate ticket allocations for vendors, crews and artists with objective criteria, membership-based models, lowering the ticket price over time, growth, and an under-26 discount. Everything was handed on to the Finance 2027 working group, which meets weekly on Mondays.

With: Daniel T (president, facilitating), Daniela (Production, timekeeper), Peter D (treasurer), Chris O, Ant H, and other ALM participants
16 October 2026ALM session

ALM — Finance overview (opening session)

Peter D’s overview of the 2026 finances at the Annual Leadership Meeting: around 5,000 transactions, the cash-flow pressure of card payments held until after the event, and the new automated expense system for 2027. The working group was announced as open to anyone who wants a say in the budget.
