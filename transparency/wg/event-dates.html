Event Dates Working Group

Status: informal group, active since July 2026Question: when should the 2027 event take place?
Mandate. Prepare the information the members need to decide the 2027 event dates — wildfire and cancellation risk, weather, bird nesting and permits, other European burns’ dates, community availability — and design the consultation that leads to the General Assembly vote on 17 September 2026. The group proposes; the members decide. Its working data and drafts live on the 2027 Dates page. Not yet formally registered under the working-groups resolution.
How this page works. Everything the group does is listed here in date order: minutes of its meetings, sync notes from working conversations between two or three of its members, and the board and community-call items where the dates decision was discussed. Sync notes are published with the agreement of the people in the conversation and record only the dates-related content.
