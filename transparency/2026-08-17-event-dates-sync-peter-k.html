A short call before that afternoon’s governance meeting. Most of it was about the governance working group; roughly ten minutes were about how the 2027 dates decision should be made, and that part produced a concrete proposal. Only the dates part is recorded here.

1. The question

Daniel T had been advocating a binding members’ vote. Over the weekend he had heard the opposite view from community members — that this is a board decision, and that a members’ vote with low turnout would lack legitimacy anyway. Peter K’s position: the board was elected and is legitimate; since the move out of July is effectively forced by the authorities’ constraints, an executive decision is defensible and the community would probably appreciate speed. Daniel T’s concern: every decision made by fiat costs a little community trust, and the organisation should be built so it stays open whoever sits on the board.

2. Where the two converged

A two-tier process: first a broad poll open to everyone (collaborators, people outside the Humans platform, other burns’ members), then the asociados express their view, and the board takes the decision on that basis — without necessarily needing a General Assembly. Peter K: “that makes more sense, especially since there is a bit of a time crunch.” (The board later opted for a binding General Assembly vote on board-preselected weeks; see the 17 and 24 August board minutes.)

On the options themselves: a small, restricted set of weeks — Daniel T’s example was last week of May, first and second week of June — rather than fifteen dates; and no “no change” option, since neither thought staying in July was viable. A veto-style question (“if it is that week, I am not coming”) was suggested for the members’ stage.

3. Other burns

Peter K reported little reaction so far from other events — none seemed especially bothered by a move — and that the Portuguese burn sees its crowd as largely separate from ours.
