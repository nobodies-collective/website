Board Meeting

Board present: Daniel T (president, facilitating), Pablo V (vice-president), Peter D (treasurer), Ward D (joined at checkout) · Absent: Chris O (secretary duties) · Also present: Daniela (production coordinator), Cocci (observer, invited by Daniel T) Following the resignations recorded at the previous meeting, the board has five members. Three attended from the start, so quorum was present.

1. Check-in and agenda

  • Check-in done. Cocci attended as an observer.
  • Agenda: production update; scheduling the general assembly and board election; moving board meetings to Thursdays; a better system for board agenda items; comms (added by Pablo V); any other business.

2. Production update

  • Permits. Daniela reported that she has identified and contacted the right person at INAGA, and has also been in touch with the local council. She is working to secure the first-choice June dates for 2027 (7–13 June, still subject to permits) on the current site, while following up with INAGA and the other permitting authorities in parallel. She expects to know more this week.
  • Daniela is also wrapping up this year’s work and will start preparing the production department’s planning for 2027 in the coming weeks.

3. General assembly and board election

  • Date. Under the statutes, the election for the vacated board seats must take place between 3 and 18 December 2026. Several dates in that window were discussed; a later date was preferred to leave time to prepare other items, such as the 2027 budget. The board approved holding a formal general assembly, including the board election, on Wednesday 16 December 2026. The three board members present (Daniel T, Pablo V, Peter D) were in favour, with no objections.
  • Seats. The election will be for the three vacated seats.
  • Voting. Voting will be ranked, as at the previous election. Voting will open online ahead of the assembly and votes can be changed until voting closes, so members do not need to attend the call to vote. The association’s new voting system will be used and tested beforehand.
  • Other items. The general assembly can also vote on other items, such as the 2027 budget. Agenda items must be announced to all members at least 14 days before the assembly, i.e. by 2 December; the full details must be available when voting opens.
  • Announcement. A formal notice will be sent to all asociados as soon as possible through the asociados mailing list, saying that there will be a general assembly with the election for three board seats, and that further agenda items may be announced later. The draft will be circulated on the board channel first.

4. Board meeting day

  • It was agreed to move the weekly board meeting from Mondays to Thursdays at 19:00, just before the community call, starting from the following week. The next board meeting is on Thursday 5 November.
  • Pablo V noted that this need not be permanent: Mondays may matter again closer to the event. The arrangement can be reviewed when new board members join.
  • Daniel T will update the calendar invitation.

5. Board agenda and task tracking

  • Daniela proposed using a ClickUp list for board agenda items, so that items arising from meetings can be turned into tasks with a visible owner. Daniel T said his priority is being able to submit agenda items in advance, see the current agenda, and add items during the meeting. Pablo V initially suggested a simple chat channel, then supported tracking tasks as well, noting that some board items had been dropped in the past; if it proves unnecessary, the board can go back to something simpler.
  • It was agreed to try Daniela’s ClickUp list. Daniela will set up an example and post it on the board channel, where it will be pinned and will replace the current Notion page.

6. Comms

  • Getting started. Pablo V asked for clarity on whether Borja can start working on comms, as Shauna B and Borja were unsure whether to wait for a coordinator selection process.
  • The board’s position. Daniel T noted that it is not the board’s role to select coordinators, and that there is no selection process yet: the organisational structure working group is still deciding whether there will be coordinator roles at all, with proposals expected the following week and a members’ vote planned by the end of November. The board members present agreed to give a positive signal: Borja is encouraged to start comms work now, without waiting for a selection process. This is not an appointment to a role; once a process is agreed, it will apply to everyone, including comms. Pablo V passed this on to Shauna B during the meeting and suggested a kickoff call open to anyone interested.
  • Projects to the end of the year. Daniela reported that, at the ALM, she had proposed two comms projects to be completed by the end of December, ahead of the launch of ticket sales in January: an event identity (name, visual identity and how it applies to socials, documents, the guide and printed material) and an outreach plan with a content calendar. She plans to contact each department this week to kick off next year’s work, which can serve as the comms kickoff, followed by a call for volunteers on Discord. Cocci also suggested a public message inviting people to join the comms team.
  • Scope of comms. Peter D raised the internal side of comms, such as regular updates to coordinators and the community (for example on Humans features). Daniel T said that, in his view, comms should focus on reaching people who are not yet in the community. Daniela noted that internal, external and association comms are distinct needs, and that a wider conversation about channels and how departments are organised should follow the organisational structure work.
  • Agreed: comms should proactively reach out to departments and working groups to find out what needs to be communicated, rather than waiting to be asked. Pablo V will pass this on to Shauna B.

7. Any other business

  • Elections working group. Daniel T is currently the only member of the elections working group and will start preparing the election. He asked board members to point him to anyone who could help and who is not planning to stand. Pablo V said he would join the working group.
  • New site. Peter D suggested asking the general assembly for a mandate on the new-site concept, since buying land would be beyond the board’s mandate and a purchase might need to happen quickly. Pablo V explained that the new-site working group is currently studying feasibility, that no spending by NCA is needed at this stage, and that any purchase would be put to an association vote. It was agreed that Pablo V will prepare a motion for the 16 December general assembly giving the new-site work a limited mandate, to be submitted before the 14-day deadline.
  • Statutes. Cocci asked how the statutes could be amended, given that a general assembly is coming up with the election. Daniel T explained that the statutes set out their own amendment process, that they are acknowledged not to be perfect, and that any proposal can be put to the general assembly; he suggested a statutes working group to gather community input first. Cocci raised two points. First, the requirement to hold an election after any board resignation: in her view the association could keep running until the next general assembly after a single resignation, with an extraordinary assembly only if several board members resign. Second, board terms: she would prefer the board to be re-elected every year, as in other associations she has been part of, so that board members can step down at a natural moment and the community can regularly renew its confidence in them. Peter D and Daniel T shared the reasoning behind the current design: staggered two-year terms, with about half the board elected each year, were chosen for continuity, including for the people on the bank account, and as a safeguard against sudden changes; the statutes also limit board service to eight years, and members can remove board members at any time by calling a general assembly. They agreed that both approaches have pros and cons and that this is for the community to decide. Cocci will consider registering a statutes working group, where these questions can be discussed openly.
