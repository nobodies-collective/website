The board discussed the governance of decision-making and the process for convening the association's first General Assembly, so that the membership can consider and endorse the board's decisions.

  • The board agreed to seek written advice from its legal and accounting advisers on which categories of decision should be brought to the General Assembly for ratification, and on the board's governance responsibilities.
  • A clear written query will be drafted and circulated for input before being sent to the advisers.
  • The first General Assembly is to be convened as soon as practical after the event, with exact timing to be decided shortly, taking the required notice period into account.

Daniela M S, as production coordinator and the point of contact with the vendor, is to be named as the authorised contact to receive the containers. The confirming communication is to be sent promptly.

5. On-Site Responsible-Person Cover

Following a request that someone empowered to speak for the event be reachable at all times, the board agreed this role need not be filled by a board member. A formally designated, suitably responsible person should be empowered to speak for the event and be reachable when needed, organised in manageable shifts. Concrete options are to be developed.

6. Membership and Quorum Administration

The board is to explore, with legal counsel, what a formal leave of absence would look like for a board member currently unable to participate, in the interest of clarity around quorum.

7. Volunteer Numbers

A positive update from volunteer coordination was noted: the collective recruitment push has materially improved numbers, with most key needs now met and only minor short windows remaining during set-up.

8. Board Engagement

Feedback that coordinators would welcome seeing more board members at coordinator meetings was noted; board members will aim to attend where helpful, while coordinators continue to lead coordination.
