A short, informal meeting held as the build period got underway, with several members already on or travelling to site. The meeting proceeded as a production and operations update; no binding resolutions were taken.

1. Check-in

Members shared brief updates, with a positive mood heading into the build. Several are on site or travelling to it.

2. Site and Production Update

The site is taking shape: the first toilets and cabins have arrived, the production village is set up with power, the first containers are in place, and water for the cantina and an initial bulk delivery have arrived. Plot flagging is progressing quickly. The first batch of toilets is on site, with the larger batch expected in roughly two weeks.

3. Container Logistics

The initial container transport underestimated the weight of the units, limiting the first day's deliveries. From the following day, a larger crane and an additional trailer allow more containers to be moved per trip, completing the work in fewer days, with cost expected to remain broadly in line with budget. An additional staff member was engaged to support the operation, within budget.

4. Accommodation

One off-site house (for up to six people) has been secured, with additional capacity being sought and terms still being finalised.

5. Local Relations and Town Visit

Board members are to visit the local town early in the event period to meet local vendors and authorities, who value seeing the board in person. Daniel T and Pablo V are to do a short visit, provisionally around 1–2 July.

6. Land Contract and Local Taxes

Efforts are underway to have the site contract signed digitally rather than in person in July. A local land tax assessment came in modestly above budget (approximately €3,000) and is to be paid, with the receipt forwarded to the local authority to progress the related documentation.

7. New Site Permitting

Preparatory permitting work for the new site is being brought forward, so that reusable documentation can be prepared and updated annually. The land was assessed as suitable; the main outstanding point is defining access roads (two separate approach roads from different directions). Proposed locations are to be provided so the consultant can begin the documentation this month.

8. Event Staffing

On the advisers' written advice, short-term event staff are to be contracted directly by the association, using recognised short-term contracting routes confirmed as appropriate by the advisers. Draft contracts are to be circulated to the advisers for review.

9. Lightning Rods on Site

Lightning-rod locations were omitted when the site map was published, so some may fall within barrio camp areas. The required clearance to containers was confirmed not to be an obstacle. Exact locations are to be measured and affected barrios informed, with the site-planning team adjusting placements where needed.

10. Finances

Finances are tracking well, with a tighter cash-flow period in July ahead of the post-event release of held funds; members agreed to cover the short-term need between them as required. Barrio contributions have come through cleanly and are held separately. Travel will be reimbursed via mileage and per diem rather than fuel cards, which is simpler and also covers those without an association card. The expense-reporting system is essentially ready for participants to submit claims.

11. Community Calls

One further community call will be held this week, after which community calls will pause until after the event.

12. Community Reception

Positive feedback was shared from members of the community, with appreciation for the work done and growing enthusiasm for next year and the new site. Overall morale was high.
