6. Finance tools in Humans

  • Finance dashboard. Peter D showed the new finance section in Humans, which is still being completed. It updates daily from the association’s payment and accounting systems and shows, among other things, the amounts owed to members awaiting reimbursement, each person’s expense reports and payments, and automatic matching of outgoing transfers to the expenses they pay. Remaining mismatches are being corrected so that a final tally of the year’s spending can be produced.
  • Who can see it. The dashboard is visible to the board and finance administrators only, because it contains personal data. The intention is to publish a summary of the year’s finances once the year is closed.
  • Budget against actual spending. The dashboard also compares the budget with actual spending, line by line. Peter D highlighted lines that came in well over budget, notably generators (largely because of fuel costs) and ice; in one case renting a generator cost more than buying one would have. Pablo V noted that this level of detail will allow better buy-versus-rent decisions in future.
  • Quotes. Because many suppliers work on quotes and send invoices later, payments sometimes leave the account before there is an invoice to match them to. Peter D plans to add a quote-approval step in Humans for next year, so that such payments are tracked until the invoices arrive.
  • Peter D also showed development statistics for Humans and reported that a Dutch translation has been added.
