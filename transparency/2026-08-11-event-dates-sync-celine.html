The second working call on the 2027 dates. About half of it was on the dates (the rest on governance-working-group matters, not recorded here). Céline S walked through the summary table she had compiled from the data page; the two then designed the three-stage consultation — two advisory polls, then the binding General Assembly vote — and set the timeline.

1. The summary table

One row per candidate week; columns for the board’s recommendation and reasoning, temperature (daily high; days over 35°C), wet days, wildfire risk and bird nesting. Hierarchy agreed: a short poll page on top, the current data page underneath, and the detailed chapters below that.

Because the nesting column is still too vague to score honestly, it will be shown as “INAGA report missing” with a disclaimer rather than a colour, so nobody picks a week purely because the nesting cell looks clean.

2. Weeks that are effectively out

  • The weekend of the Monegros Desert Festival (a 60,000-person event that rents every portable toilet in the region) — date to be confirmed against the organisers’ official announcement; provisionally 31 July 2027.
  • Any move further into July: if the reason to move is fire risk, moving later makes no sense. Those weeks stay on the list for completeness; the board expects a clear no.

3. Three stages

  • Community poll — an open form anyone can answer, including people without a Humans account and participants of other burns: favourite weeks, no-go weeks, comments.
  • Nobodies poll — the same questions on the Humans platform, for registered profiles and ticket holders, where it is one person, one vote. It will be given more weight, and that will be stated openly.
  • General Assembly vote — the only binding step: asociados rank the candidate weeks on a frozen information package.

Both polls are advisory and launch together, each explaining the whole process and pointing at the other so nobody feels asked twice. Each has a fixed deadline of about two weeks. A board recommendation is premature for the polls but will be in the final package, including how the board split.

4. Freeze rule and ranked vote

Once the General Assembly vote opens, the information package is frozen so everyone votes on the same material. Ranking allows the result to stand even if INAGA refuses the top choice: the highest-ranked approved week applies.

5. Timeline

  • 13 Aug community call: announce the plan (not launch).
  • 17 Aug governance call, then a Daniel–Céline follow-up: gather feedback on the poll drafts.
  • ~20 Aug: launch both polls for about two weeks.
  • ~3 Sept: General Assembly notice period begins; remote voting could open early if the environmental information is in hand.
  • 11 Sept at the latest: package frozen, remote voting open, to allow several days before the assembly.
  • 17 Sept: General Assembly.

Distribution of the open poll: mailing list, community call, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, and via other burns’ organisers. Members will be contacted individually about the binding vote, worded so that nobody thinks a poll answer was their vote.
