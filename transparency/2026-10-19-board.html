1. Check-in and agenda

  • Check-in done. The meeting took place on the last day of the Annual Leadership Meeting (ALM, 15–19 October).
  • Agenda: ALM highlights; board membership and the next election; secretary duties; pending membership applications; any other business.

2. ALM highlights

  • Quality of discussion. Daniel T reported that the facilitation guidelines introduced at the start by Ellen K and Cupcake made the workshops noticeably more productive, and that every agenda item produced something useful.
  • Working groups. At least half a dozen working groups are expected to come out of the ALM, each with a tangible starting point.
  • Participation. Ben D highlighted the broad range of people who proposed and facilitated sessions, and the depth of community engagement.
  • Cost. Peter D raised the cost per participant as something to look at for the next ALM. Venue and catering options were discussed; catered food was seen as worthwhile so that participants can focus on the work. No figures were confirmed and no decisions were taken.

3. Board membership and the next election

  • Announced resignations. Ben D and Victor P announced that they will resign from the board. Victor P will send his resignation in writing after the meeting; Ben D will send his later in the week, once the secretary handover is in order (see item 4).
  • Michelle. The board passed a motion, unanimously, to accept the resignation notice that Michelle gave in writing to the vice-president, Pablo V, on 5 September 2026, as her formal resignation. If Michelle indicates otherwise within seven days, the acceptance will be reconsidered. Ben D will contact her during that time. Once this takes effect, the board will have seven members and quorum will be four.
  • Effect of a resignation. Under the statutes, a resignation takes effect immediately; the departing member’s duties are assumed by the remaining board members or delegated to one of them designated by the president. An election must then be held within 45 to 60 days.
  • One election. It was agreed that it is better to hold a single election for all the vacated seats than several separate ones, so anyone intending to resign was asked to do so within a short interval of the others. It was noted that, under the statutes as they stand, elections can currently only be held to fill seats that have been vacated.
  • Candidates. The organisational structure working group is expected to publish a description of what board roles involve. Board members were encouraged to reach out actively to people they think would make good board members, including people who would not usually consider applying, and to use their questions to inform the pre-election material.
  • Statutes amendment. Peter D raised a possible statutes amendment on the term length of newly elected board members. It was agreed to treat this as a separate topic; the election does not depend on it.

4. Secretary duties

  • Daniel T, as president, designated Chris O to take on the secretary’s duties, effective from the end of this meeting, with support from Pablo V and Ben D. The main duties are handling membership applications within the required time limits and signing off minutes.
  • Ben D will hand over to Chris O before submitting his resignation.

5. Membership applications

  • The board reviewed pending membership applications.

6. Any other business

  • Departing members. Ben D and Victor P are welcome to join the start of one or two upcoming meetings, in case they are needed during the handover. Ben D said he would be available.
  • Declaration of interest. For transparency, Ben D declared an interest in possible future paid roles in recruitment and retention, or in marketing and communications, areas mentioned at the ALM as candidates for paid roles. No discussion followed.
  • Communicating the changes. Victor P asked that the board changes be communicated clearly to the community. Ben D will post a message on Discord about his decision; other departing members were invited to do the same.

7. Checkout

  • Checkout done. Board members thanked Ben D and Victor P for their work on the board this year, and also thanked Michelle.

Decisions

  • Motion passed unanimously: Michelle’s written resignation notice of 5 September 2026 is accepted as her formal resignation, unless she indicates otherwise within seven days.
  • Secretary duties designated by the president to Chris O, effective from the end of the meeting.
  • Agreed: a single election will be held for all the seats vacated, within the statutory 45–60 days.
