Start your own

To register a working group, email the Secretary, Ben D, at ben@nobodies.team with the group’s name, purpose, coordinator, expected timeline and expected output. Registration is administrative and nearly automatic: the Secretary registers the group within fourteen days, and a refusal can only be given in writing, with reasons, on the narrow grounds set out in the resolution.
