Working Groups

Working groups are how members and collaborators come together to answer a question or produce a proposal for the association. Anyone can start one.

Registered working groups

There are no formally constituted working groups yet. The board adopted the resolution and guidance on working groups on 24 August 2026 and the register opened the same evening — please check back soon.

To register a working group, email the Secretary, Ben D, at ben@nobodies.team with the group’s name, purpose, coordinator, expected timeline and expected output. Registration is administrative and nearly automatic: the Secretary registers the group within fourteen days, and a refusal can only be given in writing, with reasons, on the narrow grounds set out in the resolution.
