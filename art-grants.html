This page records the completed 2026 programme. Its grants, support and process are historical, not an offer for another edition.

What counted as “art”?

The 2026 programme welcomed intentional, shareable work, including:

  • Installations (big or small)
  • Small weird wonders (low-budget, personal, playful)
  • Mutant vehicles / artomobiles
  • Functional or “innovation” projects
  • The Temple (a dedicated reflective space)
  • Arty experiences — the more indulgent and confusing, the better!

Small projects mattered too. The programme welcomed small, strange, human-scale pieces and interactive art — not just spectacle.

Support described for 2026

The published programme described the following support:

  • Grants — typically €500–€3,000 (up to €5,000 for larger projects as an exception)
  • An Art Escort — a real human helping you make your idea feasible
  • Placement on the map + power access (if needed)
  • Artist community — shared logistics, transport, ideas
  • On-site support — tools, coordination, guidance
  • Early Entry

2026 reimbursement model: Artists were expected to pay upfront and retain invoices, bills and receipts for reimbursement. This archive does not open a new claim or application period.

2026 selection priorities

The programme prioritised:

  • Feasibility — can it actually be built?
  • Safety — will it hold up in wind, heat, rain?
  • Participation — how do people engage with it?
  • Creativity — does it feel alive, interesting, or surprising?
  • Responsibility — can you build it and remove it?

Strong idea + realistic execution beats ambition without grounding.

The 2026 process

  1. Artists developed an idea.
  2. They sketched or explained it clearly.
  3. They estimated a realistic, itemised budget.
  4. They submitted details through the 2026 art registration form, now closed.

The deadline was 17 May 2026. Applications were reviewed on a rolling basis. The round has ended.

Common misconception

Extensive experience and large-scale ideas were not prerequisites for the 2026 programme. Smaller projects were welcome. A thoughtful, doable idea suited to the realities of the playa mattered more than scale.

The Art Council’s role was to help artists shape ideas into projects that could work on site. Applications for that round are now closed.
