Elsewhere 2026 is over. If you were approved for a travel reimbursement, or came back to help with strike, here’s how to claim.


Back to Volunteering

What was this?

We offered travel expense reimbursements for volunteers who committed to early set-up or full strike. It was not a ticket discount — it was a reimbursement of actual travel costs, processed after the event. Places were limited and we prioritised volunteers for our most critical roles — infrastructure build and cantina.

Everyone bought a ticket. This covered travel costs only.

How to claim

Find your situation below and follow the steps.

Your application was approved before the event

You’re good to go. Submit your travel receipts via the Humans app:

  1. Go to humans.nobodies.team/Expenses
  2. Create a new expense report
  3. Select category: Admin / Travel Claims
  4. Add your expense lines and attach receipts to each line
  5. Submit — you’ll receive a SEPA bank transfer once approved

Your application wasn’t submitted but Daniela approved it on site

Same process — go ahead and submit via Humans:

  1. Go to humans.nobodies.team/Expenses
  2. Create a new expense report
  3. Select category: Admin / Travel Claims
  4. Add your expense lines and attach receipts to each line
  5. Submit — you’ll receive a SEPA bank transfer once approved

You came back for strike after 14 July

First, fill in the form so we have your details:

Fill in the strike form

Once you receive your approval, submit your travel receipts via humans.nobodies.team/Expenses — select Admin / Travel Claims.

Volunteers who helped with strike after 14 July will also receive a discount on their 2027 ticket. We’ll be in touch about that separately.
