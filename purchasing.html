If your department is listed above, either you send your card holder the links and details and they'll make the purchase for you, or you handle it yourself and they cover the payment. Either way, the invoicing rules, budget tracking, and shipping process in this guide apply to everyone.

The workflow

Buy what you need → get the invoice → upload it to Pleo immediately after purchase.

The easiest way: Pleo will notify you automatically. As soon as you make a purchase with your card, you'll get a push notification on your phone. Tap it, and the app takes you straight to that transaction. Attach a photo of your receipt right there.

You can also do it manually at any time:

  • Mobile app: open Pleo → Transactions → find the purchase → attach receipt
  • Web: log in at app.pleo.io → find the transaction → upload the file

That's it. No separate spreadsheet, no duplicate tracking. The receipt in Pleo is the record, and it later syncs to our accounting automatically. Please upload as you go. Don't wait until the end of the event.

Important: Meals and fuel

Association card only. Meals and fuel must be paid with an Association card. Personal payment won't be reimbursed for these categories. If you need to cover these expenses before your card arrives, contact Daniela or Peter and they'll handle it.

Your purchasing lead

Each department with a card needs a purchasing lead: the person responsible for making purchases, uploading invoices to Holded, and keeping track of spend. This can be the coordinator or someone on their team. They'll need to be added to the Finance — Purchases team in Humans.

If you don't yet have someone for this role, please let us know as soon as possible.

Tracking your budget

Check before you spend. Before making any purchase, check your department's allocated budget in Humans / Budget. Don't commit to spending beyond what's been allocated. If you think you need more, check with finance@nobodies.team before buying, not after.

Holded handles the financial record. You don't need a separate spreadsheet.

If you want to keep an internal list of what you need to buy and what's been purchased, we recommend using a simple doc or copying the on-site runner sheet. It can be as simple as a shared note or as detailed as a spreadsheet, whatever works for your team.

On-site purchases

The same card and workflow applies on site. There are two ways to handle physical purchases in town:

  • Go with the runners: join a runner trip and pay with your card directly
  • Ask a runner to buy on your behalf: add your request to the on-site runner sheet with item details, quantity, and where to buy. The runner will bring back the invoice. It's your responsibility to upload it to Holded afterwards.

For online purchases, order directly and ship to López Marín (see above).

Going through Production

If your department doesn't have a card and you need something purchased, send Daniela:

  • Item description
  • Quantity
  • Link or where to buy
  • Your budget category

Urgent needs while cards are being set up: if you can pay personally, do so and submit an expense report in Humans later and we'll reimburse you. If you can't front the cost, contact Daniela or Peter directly and we'll sort it.

No invoice, no reimbursement. Missing or incorrect invoices mean we can’t process the reimbursement.

Already purchased and don’t have an invoice? It can be requested after purchase, please do so.

When in doubt, ask us.
