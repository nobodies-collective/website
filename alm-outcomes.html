Friday 16 October

Welcome & Year in Review
⚡ All togetherFriday 16 October

What this session was

The opening session of ALM 2026 — a welcome, a practical briefing, and a warm-up to get everyone oriented before the working weekend began.

What happened

Welcome

Daniela welcomed attendees and introduced the org team: Doreen (venue), Yllza (meals & shifts), Jakir and Martian (equipment & tech), Céline and Daniela (programme), Cupcake (ALM finance and travel reimbursements), and Leaf/Frank supporting remotely during the weekend. Doreen ran through house rules for the Vidalia space.

Lessons Learned

Céline opened a short lessons-learned round, drawing on the Discord channel and inviting the room to share experiences from 2026. Stories ranged from “emergency mud baths” and lost Jeep keys to sound policy breaches by a certain Trojan horse; setting a candid, energetic tone for the weekend.

Annual Roadmap framework

Daniela then presented the proposed Annual Roadmap framework: a film-industry-inspired production stage model (Development → Pre-production → On-site → Post-event) with hard milestones: event dates confirmed and budget voted by December, tickets on sale in January, barrio placement moving earlier in the year. This generated good discussion, particularly around how early barrio leads could realistically engage and how earlier ticket sales would help cash flow.

Finance Overview

Peter gave a Finance Overview of the 2026 cycle: ~5,000 transactions, a 20% margin in the first year as a new entity, and a cash flow crunch caused by Stripe holding a significant portion of revenue until after the event. A new automated finance system (expense reports → approval → accounting → bank) is now in place for 2027. The Finance Working Group kicks off the following Monday, open to anyone who wants a say in the budget.

Asset Acquisition Working Group

Ellen briefed the room on the Asset Acquisition Working Group: NCA leased the containers for the 2026 event; the goal now is to separate the event-time line from the acquisition process. Next steps would be putting in place a lease for the material assets for the 2027 event, and to complete a dossier for GNSL as the first step toward formal acquisition. All communications go through the working group; single point of contact with GNSL is Ben Ford.

Facilitation Guidelines briefing

Cupcake and Ellen closed with a Facilitation Guidelines briefing for the weekend’s sessions: roles (facilitator, secretary/timekeeper, hybrid guardian), five principles (autonomy, speak with intention, one point per person, speak for yourself, be for not against), hand signals for speaking order, clarifications, disagreement, and temperature checks, plus a consensus model (agree / agree with reservations / block) rather than majority voting.

Outcomes / decisions

  • Annual Roadmap stage framework proposed; turned into a Gantt chart post-ALM
  • Finance Working Group confirmed, starting Monday 19 October, open to all
  • Asset Acquisition Working Group update shared; next step is GNSL dossier
  • Facilitation framework distributed to all session leads for the weekend
