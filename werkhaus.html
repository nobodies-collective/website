What was Werkhaus in 2026?

The 2026 programme described Werkhaus as the barrio for crew arriving early to build, staying through strike, volunteering during the event, or holding a year-round role.

It was conceived as a shared home for people building, eating and solving problems together.

The 2026 programme welcomed first-time participants as well as experienced crew.

What the 2026 programme included

Meals

The published arrangement covered breakfast, lunch and dinner at Cantina during the crew member’s stay.

Space & shade

Barrio infrastructure, shade structures, and camping space in the crew home.

Your people

A community of experienced, committed people who know how to get things done.

Early entry

The arrangement included being on site before the event gates opened.

€50 2026 barrio fee, described as covering drinks and extras during the stay

2026 eligibility

The 2026 programme required one of the following commitments:

Pre-event

Set-up crew

A commitment to at least one week on site during set-up before the gates opened.

Event time

Volunteer shifts

20 hours of volunteer shifts during the event, or 16 hours in critical roles (Malfare, site leads, Nomads, event maintenance or fluffing).

Year-round

Organisational roles

A year-round organisational role within the NCA.

Travel support described for 2026

The 2026 programme described reimbursement of eligible travel expenses for volunteers arriving early for set-up or staying through full strike. This was separate from the €50 barrio fee and was not a ticket discount.

2026 application process — closed

The 2026 application process is retained here as a record, not an invitation to submit a new application. Confirmations were scheduled from mid-May.

  1. Applicants needed a ticket for Elsewhere 2026.
  2. They registered on Humans and selected the relevant shifts.
  3. They completed the Werkhaus application form for that edition.

The 2026 application form is no longer linked here because that cycle has ended.

The published 2026 policy described reimbursement of actual travel costs after the event. No future reimbursement programme is promised by this archive.
